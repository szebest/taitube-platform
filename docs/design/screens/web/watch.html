Liner notes

412,301 views · 2 days ago · #hi8 #lisbon #nightwalk

Every night for a month I walked the same loop through Alfama and Graça with a Hi8 camcorder I found at a flea market for eight euros. No stabiliser, no grade, no music I didn't record on the street. The dropouts and the tracking wobble are left in on purpose: that is what the tape remembers. The tram sequence at is the one people ask about; how I captured the tapes on a 2009 desktop is at . Recorded between 23:00 and 05:00, January and February. Thank you to the night staff at the Graça kiosk for the coffee and for not asking questions. Gear: the camcorder, two batteries, a lens cloth. That's it.
