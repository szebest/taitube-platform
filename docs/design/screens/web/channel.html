About

I film cities at the hour they stop performing. After the last tram, before the first bakery, when a street is only a street.

Everything here is shot on a camcorder older than some of you, on tapes I buy in batches of ten. The grain is not a filter. The dropouts are not a choice. I keep them because they are what the night looked like to that machine.

No music unless the place made it. No narration unless I was there to say it. If a film is slow, it is slow on purpose; sit with it.
